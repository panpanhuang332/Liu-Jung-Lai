### 2.5.2 The sensemaking loop and cue extraction

Sensemaking proceeds by extracting cues from an equivocal environment and elaborating them into plausible accounts, iteratively (Weick, 1995; Maitlis & Christianson, 2014). Pirolli and Card (2005) formalize an analogous structure in analytic work: cycles of information foraging and sense-making in which evidence is gathered, hypotheses are formed, and representations are revised. The transfer of this model is analogical and deliberately bounded: Pirolli and Card describe professional analysts foraging external documents, not employees interpreting their own roles, and the analogy is invoked here only for the iterative gather--hypothesize--revise structure it shares with organizational accounts of sensemaking as ongoing and cyclical (Weick, 1995; Maitlis & Christianson, 2014). So bounded, it supports the loop structure the paper proposes---initial role interpretation, employee--AI interaction, AI-generated cue production, and updated appraisal, with the update conditioning the next probe. The AI-interactive role sensemaking loop is a specific case of this iterative process in which the cue source is the artifact's own outputs and the hypothesis under continual revision is the meaning of the technology for one's own role.
