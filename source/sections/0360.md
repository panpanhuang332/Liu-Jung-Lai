## 3.3 Components of the two role appraisals

Each appraisal is specified through named components, so that an appraisal can be *partial*---endangering some role components while leaving others intact, or opening some while others feel threatened. This componential structure matters for the propositions in Section 4, because different cues bear on different components: a demonstration of a core task speaks to occupational identity and the market value of expertise, whereas a restructuring announcement speaks to employment security. Table 2 lists the components. Longer-horizon *employability* is treated as a related but distinct concern rather than as a fifth threat component, because it is appraised against the external labor market rather than against the present role.

**Table 2.** *Components of role threat and role opportunity appraisal.*

  -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Component                                                  What is appraised
  ---------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ***Role threat appraisal --- endangered components***      

  Employment security                                        Whether one's job or headcount is at risk.

  Market value of expertise                                  Whether the market value of one's skills is being eroded.

  Role autonomy                                              Whether discretion and control over how one works are being narrowed or standardized.

  Occupational identity and status                           Whether the distinctiveness, meaning, and standing of one's role are being downgraded.

  ***Role opportunity appraisal --- enabling components***   

  Role augmentation                                          Whether AI widens the range of tasks one can accomplish.

  Role elevation                                             Whether AI absorbs lower-value tasks, shifting one toward judgment, relational, creative, or strategic work.

  Role reinvention                                           Whether AI capability can be built into a redefined professional identity and a new basis of role value.
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
