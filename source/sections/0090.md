## 1.5 Structure of the paper

Section 2 reviews the theoretical background and grounds the two mechanisms, developing the pragmatic and discourse foundations of narrative reversal alongside the enactment, cue-extraction, and cue-credibility perspectives that underpin recursive sensemaking. Section 3 develops the conceptual framework, distinguishing mechanisms from constructs, boundary conditions, and contextual antecedents, and formalizing the appraisal components and the taxonomy of adoption response modes. Section 4 presents propositions organized around the two mechanisms. Section 5 discusses theoretical contributions, boundary conditions, and directions for empirical research. Section 6 concludes.
