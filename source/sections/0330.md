# 3. Conceptual Framework

Section 2 assembled the theoretical materials for the paper's two mechanisms. This section formalizes the constructs those mechanisms require and specifies how they connect. To avoid restating Section 2's arguments and to keep the framework legible, the exposition is deliberately compact and organized around one figure and three tables. Section 3.1 states the overall architecture (Figure 1). Section 3.2 distinguishes mechanisms from measurable constructs and sorts the framework's elements into three tiers---primary mechanisms and constructs, contextual antecedents, and boundary conditions---together with the terminal outcome (Table 1). Section 3.3 specifies the components of the two role appraisals (Table 2). Section 3.4 formalizes the outcome as a taxonomy of adoption response modes (Table 3). Section 4 then states the propositions that link these constructs.
