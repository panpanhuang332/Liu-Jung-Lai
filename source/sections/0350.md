## 3.2 Mechanisms, constructs, and tiers

Table 1 defines each element in condensed form, assigns it to a tier, states its position in the model, and points to the subsection that carries its full definition; to avoid the verbatim duplication a table of full definitions would entail, the table entries are deliberately abbreviated.

One terminological clarification governs the primary tier. Two of its entries---enablement narrative backfire and the AI-interactive role sensemaking loop---are **mechanisms**: specified processes that connect constructs, not measurable states. The remaining primary entries---the AI-as-substitute frame and the two role appraisals---are **constructs** in the measurement sense. The distinction matters empirically because the two are treated differently in research design: constructs are operationalized and measured directly (Section 5.3 provides illustrative items), whereas mechanisms are tested indirectly, through the propositions that link their constituent constructs (P1--P3 for backfire; P5--P7 for the loop). Table 1 labels the tiers accordingly, and the framework consistently refers to backfire and the loop as mechanisms rather than constructs.
