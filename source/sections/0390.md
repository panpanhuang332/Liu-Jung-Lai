# 4. Propositions

This section states the framework's propositions. Consistent with the layered causal structure developed in Sections 2 and 3, they are conditional rather than bivariate: each specifies the conditions under which a link holds and, where relevant, the boundary condition that moderates it. They are organized around the two mechanisms---narrative reversal (Sections 4.1--4.2) and recursive cue production (Section 4.3)---and close with the mapping from appraisal to response mode (Section 4.4). Because role threat can issue in more than one response, the propositions are stated to differentiate response modes rather than to predict a single level of adoption.
